How we did this

1
Same 7 days for everyone
Every station was measured over the same week: Nov 6-13, 2026.
2
Public view counts only
We used the numbers anyone can see on each post. No inside data.
3
Reels and video only on Facebook
Facebook only shows public view counts on video, so that is what we counted.
4
Medians over averages
The median is the middle post. One viral hit cannot bend it the way it bends an average.
5
Viral flag at one third
If a single post made more than a third of a week's views, we flagged it.
YouTube numbers were pulled with yt-dlp on Nov 13, 2026. YouTube videos less than 24 hours old were left out of averages and medians because views were still coming in. X is follower counts only.
