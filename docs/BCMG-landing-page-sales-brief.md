## 7. Conversion Flow (how a lead is captured)

- **Primary CTA:** "Ready To Speak With Patrick?" lead form — "Tell us about your business and a team member will reach out shortly."
- **Fields (all required):** Name, Company, Email, Phone, "What are you interested in?" (Video Production / Marketing / Marketing Consulting / A little of everything / Not sure yet, let's talk), and a short "about your business" note.
- **On submit, three things fire:** (1) email to the team, (2) a text/SMS + contact creation via GoHighLevel automation, (3) a Meta Pixel "Lead" event. Then it redirects to a thank-you page. *(All three confirmed working.)*
- **Secondary CTAs:** "See Our Packages", "See Our Work", per-tier "Get Started", and "Apply For A Spot" (Fractional CMO).
- **Landing-page lockdown:** no top nav and no footer site links, so the only path forward is the form.
