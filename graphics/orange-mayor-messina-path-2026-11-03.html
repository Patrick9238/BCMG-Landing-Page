Benchmark: in 2022, the GOP’s best modern year, DeSantis got 46.1% in Orange. Messina has to beat that in an electorate that is bluer than 2022’s.
His only real path
  1. Max out Election Day Republicans.
    141,596 Orange Republicans have not voted yet.
  2. Win NPAs about 60 to 40.
    191,165 NPA/Minor voters left, the biggest pool in the county.
  3. Flip nearly 1 in 5 Democrats.
    Targets: Murphy’s moderates (26.2%) and Uribe’s voters (14.7%); the police union backed Uribe.
Plausible?
Only if all three happen at once. A long shot, not impossible.
